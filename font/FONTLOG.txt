FONTLOG for Aleua Sans
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Aleua Sans font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Aleua Sans fonts
and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Aleua Sans is a rounded geometric monoline typeface designed for the
visual identity of Asociación ALEUA (Asociación de Lazos Europeos y
Acción), Mengíbar, Jaén, Spain.

The letterforms are derived from the ALEUA logotype (A, L, E, U) and
extended into a full Latin character set for Spanish and Western
European languages. The family currently contains one style, Bold, in
OpenType/CFF (.otf) and TrueType (.ttf) formats.

The fonts are generated from Python sources (see sources/) and are
released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (see OFL.txt).


ChangeLog
---------

31 October 2026 (Asociación ALEUA) Aleua Sans v1.400
- Redesigned figures to match the letters. All figures share one width
  (tabular, 560 units, so they do not shift in clocks and tables) and
  one bowl radius:
  - 0 is a stadium with straight sides.
  - 1 has a horizontal base and a straight flag.
  - 2 has an open bowl that starts at the height of the flag of 1 and
    a straight diagonal tangent to it.
  - 3 and 5 have flat tops and a square join into the bowl, as in P
    and R; the top bar of 5 meets the stem at a square corner, as the
    bars of F.
  - 6 has a straight stroke tangent to its bowl, and 9 is the 6 turned
    by 180 degrees.
  - 4 and 7 are made of straight strokes; 0 and 8 are symmetric.
- Copyright and authorship set to Asociación ALEUA in the font
  metadata, OFL.txt and AUTHORS.txt.
- The ss01 A is built in its own module (sources/aleua_logo_a.py).

31 October 2026 (Asociación ALEUA) Aleua Sans v1.300
- The ss01 A is now the A of the ALEUA logotype: the open A with the
  logotype swoosh and the gap where it crosses the right leg, measured
  from the imagotype artwork (sources/logo_swoosh.json).
- TrueType rounding keeps the slope of straight edges: end points of
  diagonal lines are rounded to the integers that best preserve each
  exact angle, so parallel stroke edges stay parallel in the TTF.

31 October 2026 (Asociación ALEUA) Aleua Sans v1.200
- Redesigned the at sign (@) as a single-storey a wrapped by an open
  ring.
- Added the middle dot (U+00B7).
- Narrower circumflex and dieresis over I and i; accented glyphs are
  widened when an accent would overhang its neighbours.
- Exact mirror and rotational symmetry for symmetric glyphs.
- Exact parallel stroke edges in the OTF (fractional coordinates).
- Unified optical spacing and kerning model; improved uppercase to
  lowercase transitions.
- Added copyright, license and description metadata.

31 October 2026 (Asociación ALEUA) Aleua Sans v1.100
- Outlines fitted with cubic Bézier curves, with nodes on extrema,
  corners and line joins; OTF (CFF) output added alongside TTF.

30 October 2026 (Asociación ALEUA) Aleua Sans v1.000
- Initial release: stroke weight, corner radii and proportions measured
  from the ALEUA logotype; B, D, P and R with square joins; shorter
  middle arm of E and F; tabular figures; kerning; stylistic sets ss01
  (logo swoosh A) and ss02 (open A).
